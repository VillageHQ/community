# Demo pictures

Everyone and every company here is made up.

## Faces (`faces/`)

Every face is a portrait of a person who does not exist. None is a photo, and none was made from a photo of
anyone: each was generated from a text prompt alone, then cropped to 256 × 256 and saved as webp.

- **Source:** generated for this project with Stable Diffusion XL base 1.0 (`stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0`)
  and its fp16-fixed autoencoder (`madebyollin/sdxl-vae-fp16-fix`, MIT), 30 steps, guidance 6, 1024 × 1024.
- **Licence:** the model's licence (CreativeML Open RAIL++-M) claims no rights in what it generates. This project
  releases these pictures under the repository's licence (AGPL-3.0), like the rest of `public/`.
- **Prompt:** `professional corporate headshot photo of a <description>, head and shoulders, centered, looking at the
  camera, friendly natural expression, soft diffused studio light from the left, plain warm light grey background,
  85mm lens, realistic skin texture, sharp focus`, with a negative prompt against drawings, renders, text and
  celebrities. Each picture was checked by eye for a likeness to a known person.
