# Your data

Every member can see what the community keeps about them and where, take it with them as one JSON file, choose who
can reach them, and delete their account for real.

- **The page** (`/members/data`): what is stored and where; what Village receives; the consent record (fill-in,
  network sharing, accept intro asks with date and where, partner report credit, directory, message policy).
- **Switches:** hide me from the directory; who can message me (anyone / only people I was introduced to); don't
  look me up; share my network (on Network); accept intro asks.
- **Export:** the member's own account, card, choices, consent, posts, replies, reactions, asks, offers and answers
  they wrote, intros they are part of (their own notes only), messages they sent, email settings, weekly emails and
  their own audit actions. Never another member's email, private notes or messages.
- **Delete:** after typing "delete". Removes the account's sign-in, card, photo, tags, filled-in lines, choices,
  blocks, network link, posts, replies, reactions, asks, offers, answers, the words of their messages, invites,
  outbox emails to or naming them, weekly emails, usage counters and rate limits; asks Village to take them out of
  the group and cancel their Village user. Intros stay as anonymous rows, deleted asks as anonymous tallies, so the
  partner report keeps its numbers (an open intro becomes withdrawn). Audit and moderation copies keep no personal
  text. An admin can run the same delete from Manage. The only admin can't delete themselves.
- **Onboarding:** "Can members ask you for intros?" is a required yes/no step when intros are on. Members who joined
  earlier keep being askable until they choose.
