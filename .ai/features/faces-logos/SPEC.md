# Faces and company logos

Members see each other's faces, and each company's logo, wherever a person or a company appears. The pictures come
from the profile fill-in (Village's public profile of the member), with the same consent as every other filled-in line.

- **Face:** the member's uploaded photo, else the face public profiles found, else the initials tile. Shown in the
  header and Today (asks you could help with, waiting intros, New this week), the directory and profiles, asks and
  their answers and offers, intros, messages, the feed, network connectors and paths, the Meet and welcome strips,
  and Admin → Members.
- **Company logo:** a small logo before the company name on the directory, profiles, New this week, ask rows and the
  members' job board. It shows only while the company on show is the one public profiles found: the member typed no
  company, or typed the same one (any case, outer spaces), and the filled-in company line is not hidden. No company
  shown, no logo. The logo never stands in for a face. On the job board a job's company gets the logo of an active
  member whose company on show is that company, the member who shared the board first.
- **Outside people:** network search results, the connectors on a path and the person being reached show the picture
  Village sends (`image_url` on search, `avatar` on paths); a member among them shows their own face.
- **Company website:** the fill-in also keeps the company's domain as a "Company website" line (listed on the card's
  fill-in panel and in the export, shown nowhere else).
- **Safe links only:** a picture is shown only from an https link or the app's own `/demo/...` drawings.
- **Your data:** the export lists the face and logo links and whether each is on show.
- **Demo:** drawn made-up faces and company marks in `public/demo/`. Demo mode seeds the fill-in for every made-up
  member (Lena said "Don't look me up" and keeps her initials; public profiles still list Grace at her last company,
  so her card shows no logo), and its canned Village answers carry the same pictures. The test fake Village does too.
- **Not done, on purpose:** emails and the public job board stay text-only; the fund's own logo is the Settings one.
