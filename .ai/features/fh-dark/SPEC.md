# Dark mode: a dark version of Founderhall's look

**Status:** built (this pull request). Charter: `CHARTER.md`. How it works: `NOTES.md`.

Community keeps a dark mode. It is Founderhall's look in the dark: a warm charcoal page, sheets one step lighter,
hairline rules, warm paper-coloured text in the same tiers as light, and the fund colour lightened until it reads at
4.5:1. Every text pair meets WCAG AA. The switch is a row on Your data; without a choice the page follows the system.
Light mode does not change.
