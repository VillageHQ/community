# Search before connecting your own network

Any signed-in member can search on Meet at once, as soon as Village is set up and the community group exists. Before
they connect, the search covers the networks other members chose to share. Paths run only through those members.
Adding their own network is an invitation shown with the results ("Add your network so others can reach people you
know"), never a gate.

- **Before connecting:** the search, the paths and an intro ask through a member who shared all work. The heading
  says "N people members can reach", and the page shows no network settings.
- **The invitation:** "Add your network" opens the same steps as before: LinkedIn, the sharing consent, the sync.
  "Not now" on any step leaves nothing shared, and Meet shows the search again.
- **After connecting:** searches run as the member, so their own contacts count. Results and paths found before are
  cleared.
- **Consent is unchanged:** a member's own network is searched and shared only after they agree and sync. A member
  who refused or left is never a path. Leaving the group still stops sharing; the member can still search what
  others share.
- **Still gated:** no Village keys, no group yet, an unconfirmed email, or a Village link waiting for an admin.
- **When Village says no:** if Village won't search the shared networks for someone who never synced, the member sees
  "Searching the community's networks isn't available right now. Add your network to search the people you know."
