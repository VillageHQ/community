## Vision
Every fund's founder community runs on warm introductions. This app makes the community's combined network
searchable and turns it into introductions that both people agreed to, with numbers the fund's partners can trust.

## Mission
Give any venture fund (and later any member community) an open-source community app it can host itself in one
command, or have run for it, that founders use mostly from email and their phone.

## The problem
Portfolio directories go stale. Founders can't see who in the community knows the person they need. Intro requests
get lost in email and chat threads, and the fund can't show its partners what the community did.

## Who it is for
- **The fund's platform team** (the admin): invites founders, runs the community, sends the partner report.
- **Portfolio founders** (members): ask for help and intros, answer the asks they can help with, mostly by email.
- **People outside the community** asked for an intro: they say yes or no thanks on one page, with no account.
- **The fund's partners**: read the partner report; they never need to sign in.

More detail: `.ai/personas.md`.

## Non-goals
- A public social network. The feed exists but is a switch, off by default.
- Showing members each other's contacts: search shows paths through members who agreed to share, never an address
  book. Admins see intro counts and outcomes, never who asked whom.
- Doing the introduction for people: the connector always decides, and the person asked always says yes first.

## How success shows
- Intro requests get a yes from both sides and happen; the asker says the intro helped.
- Asks get an answer within days, not lost in a chat channel.
- Members keep their cards current without being chased; most answer the weekly email.
- The fund sends a partner report every quarter built only from real counts.
