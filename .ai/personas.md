## The platform lead (admin)
Runs the fund's founder community next to many other jobs. Invites a portfolio of 50 to 500 founders, often from a
spreadsheet. Wants it running in an afternoon, wants to switch off what the fund doesn't use, and needs one report
each quarter to show the partners the community is worth it. Reads on a laptop at work and a phone between meetings.
Worried about: founders' data, email landing in spam, a tool nobody opens.

## The busy founder (member)
Runs a seed or Series A company. Opens the app rarely; lives in email. Needs a customer, a hire or an investor intro
a few times a quarter, and will help others when asked directly and briefly. Answers the weekly email from a phone.
Hates passwords, long forms and being pitched.
Worried about: being spammed with asks, their network being exposed.

## The connector (member)
A founder or operator with a strong network who gets asked for intros often. Wants to say no as easily as yes, to
check with the other person first, and to never be named in a report without agreeing.
Worried about: burning goodwill, a weekly flood of requests.

## The person asked for an intro (outside the community)
A customer, investor or candidate who doesn't know the app exists. Gets one email in the connector's name, decides in
a few seconds on one page with no account, and can stop all future asks from this community.
Worried about: being added to a list.

## The partner (reader of the report)
A partner at the fund. Reads the partner report by email or as a printout: intros asked, made, said to help, and
what they led to. Never signs in.
