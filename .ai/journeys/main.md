# Main journey: a founder gets a warm intro both sides agreed to

> ⚠ RECONSTRUCTED from code on 2026-11-10 — needs confirmation
> Sources: `src/platform/http/routes.ts`, `src/app/(app)/page.tsx`, `src/modules/intros/AGENTS.md`, the journeys below.

Who: a portfolio founder (member) in a fund's community, the member who knows the person (connector), the person
asked for (often outside the community), and the fund's platform lead (admin).

1. The admin invites the founder → the founder joins from the invite link and fills in their card
   ([admin-invite-and-import.md](admin-invite-and-import.md)).
2. The founder signs in with an emailed link → lands on Today, which lists only what needs them this week
   ([sign-in.md](sign-in.md), [today.md](today.md)).
3. The founder searches the network for the person they need → sees a path through a member who agreed to share →
   asks that member for an intro with one note ([ask-for-an-intro.md](ask-for-an-intro.md)).
4. The connector says yes → the person asked gets one private link and says yes too → the connector joins them
   ([answer-an-intro.md](answer-an-intro.md)).
5. Two weeks later the founder is asked "did it help?" → the answer counts in the fund's partner report
   ([partner-report.md](partner-report.md)). Payoff: a warm intro that happened, and a number the partners believe.

Other core journeys: [asks-board.md](asks-board.md), [weekly-email.md](weekly-email.md), [your-data.md](your-data.md).
