PRWhat is this, exactly?

Your portfolio's own community, built on Village's network.

Village maps who people already know. Community uses it so a founder can ask for a warm intro instead of writing cold. The rest is a private place for your founders to talk.

  • Feed

    Founders ask the room, share a win or offer a hand. Comments and reactions are built in, and admins can moderate.

  • Members

    A directory of everyone in the community, with the profile each person fills in.

  • Messages

    One-to-one conversations between two members.

  • Network

    Each member searches their own network plus the relationships other members share, through Village. It isn't a search of everyone in the world.

  • Intros

    A member asks a fellow member for an intro with a short note. That person says yes or passes.

Your ask is on its way

Here's what happens next.

  1. Jonas says yes or passes. He can say no as freely as yes. If he does nothing, the ask closes by itself after 14 days.

  3. Jonas marks it as introduced. You both see where the ask stands in Intros.

PRWhat does it cost?

The software is free. You pay your host and Village directly.

  • The software

    Free

    MIT licensed. No seat fee and no contract.

  • Hosting and database

    Your host's price

    Your host bills you, not us. Check their current prices before you pick one.

  • Network search and intros

    Your Village credits

    The app estimates about $0.22 for a full search and $0.10 for a path lookup, at $0.02 a credit, so 100 searches come to about $22. Village's prices can change. Village's setup asks for a payment method before your app goes live.

  • Feed, members and messages spend no credits. Only network search and intros use Village.
  • You set a daily limit. Each member gets 50 searches and path lookups a day by default. You can set it from 1 to 500.
  • You see the usage. Admin, Credits shows use per day and per member. Your balance lives on Village's platform.
  • Email is optional. To send invites and intro notices by email, add your own Resend account. Resend bills you for it.

PRIs my founders' data safe? And ours?

Here's where it lives and who can see it.

Your database

The community's data is yours.

Profiles, posts, messages and intro notes sit in your own Postgres database, on your host's account.

Village

Network search runs on Village.

A member who shares gives Village their email address and connects their network there. Your Village keys sit in your database as plain text, so be careful who can open it.

Founders read the deal before they share.

These are the app's own words. Each member ticks a box to agree. "Not now" keeps the feed, messages and members on.

You share

Joining shares your Village network with every current and future member of this community. They can find your contacts and ask you for introductions.

You get

You gain access to the networks of the other members in return. Search them and see who can introduce you, and why.

  • You say yes or no to every intro anyone asks you for.
  • That covers intros only. Without asking you each time, members can find your contacts in search and see you as someone who can introduce them.
  • Your email address goes to Village, the company that runs network search for this community.
  • You can leave the group from this page. That stops sharing with the group, but people who already saw your contacts can't unsee them.
  • If an admin removes you from this site, we also ask Village to take you out of the group. If Village can't, you stay shared until you leave inside Village.

  • Can admins read direct messages?

    Not in the app. No page, route or role reaches them. An admin sees a copy of a message only when the person who received it reports it.

  • The limit you should know

    Messages aren't end-to-end encrypted. Anyone with access to your database can read them, backups included. Tell your members who runs it.

  • Who sees an intro ask?

    Only the person asking and the person asked. Admins see counts, plus a copy of a note if the person asked reports it.

  • Does anyone get contacted without a yes?

    The app never contacts the person being introduced. The member who knows them decides, then sends the note themselves.

  • Who can get in?

    Only people you invite. Public sign-up is closed. Each link is personal, works once and lasts 14 days. Network search stays off until the member's email is confirmed.

  • Your network is in it too

    Creating the group shares your own Village network with every current and future member. Decide that before you click.

We make no compliance claims for your copy, because you choose where it runs. Found a problem? Report it privately.

What running your own copy means

  • The code is yours to keep. It's MIT licensed, and it runs on your host's account.
  • You choose when to update. Updates aren't automatic. We fix the latest code, but we can't patch your copy for you.
  • Village is optional. Only network search and intros need it. Your feed, members and messages run without it.
  • Help comes through GitHub. There's no managed plan or support contract. Open an issue there.

A few more questions

Do founders get email notifications?

With email on, invites and intro notices go by email. Direct messages and feed posts send no email, so founders see them when they open the app.

Can it run on our own domain?

Your host gives your site an address, and hosts let you attach your own domain there. The app reads its public address from a setting called APP_URL. You also give Village that address in the checklist.

What happens when I remove someone?

They're signed out, can't sign back in and leave the directory. You choose whether their posts stay or go. If they're in the Village group, we ask Village to take them out and tell you what happened. If Village can't, their network stays shared until they leave inside Village.

Does Village host it for me?

No. You run your own copy on your own host. Village builds the project and sells the credits that network search uses.

What if someone forgets their password?

With email on, they reset it from the sign-in page. Admins can't set a member's password, on purpose: it would let an admin sign in as them and read their messages. The README covers email off.
