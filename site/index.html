The shortest way from your founder to the right person.

Founderhall is a private home for your portfolio, with your fund's name on the door. A founder opens it, sees what needs them this week, finds who in your community can introduce them, and asks. Nobody is introduced without saying yes.

Fund first

Your fund's name on the door. Your founders' networks behind it.

Members see your community's name, logo and colour. Through Village, members who choose to share connect their networks, and everyone in the community can search them together: who can introduce me to this person, and how do they know them?

  • Invite only

    Only people you invite get in. Each link is personal, works once and lasts 14 days. Invite one person, or many from a pasted list or a CSV.

  • Search the shared networks

    A founder searches their own network and the ones other members share. Community members come first, with a plain-words reason for how each one knows the person.

  • One ask reaches the people who can help

    When a founder posts an ask, the members on a path to the person it names, and up to 5 whose "can help with" matches, each get one email. They can offer an intro or answer.

  • A directory that fills itself in

    Filter members by stage, sector, cohort and how recently their card was updated. Cards can fill in from public work profiles once you record what allows it; a member's own words always win.

Double opt-in intros

Four steps, and a yes at the two that matter.

The connector agrees, then the person your founder wants to meet agrees, and only then are they joined.

  1. 1

    Sam asks, with a short note

    From the path to Alex, Sam asks Priya, the member who knows them. Alex hears nothing yet.

  2. 2

    Priya decides

    Yes, or pass. A pass is private, and a reason, if she gives one, stays with her.

  3. 3

    Alex decides

    Priya forwards a private link, or has the app email it. One page, no account: yes, no thanks, or stop all intro requests.

  4. 4

    One message joins them

    The ask closes as introduced. Two weeks later Sam is asked whether it helped.

Nobody is introduced without saying yes.

  • Sam can't tell whose no it was. He sees a kind, neutral message, and who else can reach Alex.
  • Nobody gets chased forever. An ask nobody answers closes by itself after 14 days. If the app emailed Alex the link, one reminder goes 4 days later, and that's the only one.
  • The giving side is protected. Each founder has a weekly allowance of asks, 5 by default, and a connector gets at most 10 intro asks a week. A declined ask gives its place back.
  • Alex's yes can't be faked by a link preview. Opening the page changes nothing; only a button press answers it. The link works once and closes with the ask.
  • Stop means stop. "Stop all intro requests" closes the asks waiting on Alex's answer, and holds back new ones.

For you and your partners

Every intro, counted for the partner meeting.

The asks your founders make become a report your partners can read: asked, each side's yes, introduced, declined, expired, and whether it helped.

  • Nothing to fill in

    Every step of every intro is counted as it happens. This quarter so far, last quarter and since launch sit side by side.

  • The no's are shown too

    Declines, expiries and withdrawals sit beside the yeses. Declines are split by who said no, and expiries by who never answered. A count that only goes up teaches the wrong habits.

  • Counts, not gossip

    Admins see totals, never who asked whom. A connector is named only if they turned on "Credit me in reports".

  • Print it, download it, send it

    Print the page, download a CSV, or email it to up to 20 partners. Sending your first report is the last step of setup.

Trust

Whose data is this? Here's where it lives and who can see it.

  • The community's data is yours

    Profiles, asks, intros and messages sit in your own Postgres database, on your host's account.

  • Network search runs on Village

    A member who shares gives Village their email address and connects their network there. With a setup token set, your Village secret key is sealed in your database.

  • Nobody is contacted without a yes

    The member who knows them decides first. The person being introduced hears nothing until then, and isn't introduced until they say yes too.

  • Admins can't read direct messages

    Not in the app: no page, route or role reaches them. An admin sees a copy of a message only when the person who received it reports it.

  • Not end-to-end encrypted

    Messages and intro notes are stored as plain text. Anyone with access to your database can read them, backups included. Tell your members who runs it.

  • Your network is in it too

    Creating the group shares your own Village network with every current and future member. Decide that before you click.

  • Anyone can say "Don't look me up"

    Filled-in cards say where each line came from. A member can hide any filled-in line, or ask not to be looked up at all.

  • We make no compliance claims for your copy

    You choose where it runs. Found a problem? Report it privately.

Your choice

Start quiet. Turn on more when your community wants it.

Admin, Features switches the optional parts on or off. Off, a part's pages and its menu entry are gone.

  • On

    Members, messages, asks, network search and intros. On from the start. Search and intros need Village; the rest runs without it.

  • Off

    Feed. A shared feed for asks, wins and offers, with replies and reactions.

  • Off

    Jobs. Founders link their hiring tool and the jobs update every hour. Add a public board and a widget for your website.

  • Off

    Slack and Discord. New asks and a weekly line in your channel, and /ask from Slack.

  • Off

    Perks. A perks page for your founders.

What you pay for

The software is free. You pay your host and Village directly.

  • The software

    Open source under AGPL-3.0. No seat fee and no contract.

  • Hosting and database

    Your host bills you, not us. Check their current prices before you pick one.

  • Network search and intros

    They spend your Village app's credits, as does filling in cards from public profiles. Members, messages and the feed spend none.

  • A monthly cap

    Lookups stop at the community's monthly credit cap, and every admin gets an email at 80% and at 100%.

  • A daily limit

    Each member gets 50 searches and path lookups a day by default. You set it from 1 to 500.

  • You see the use

    Admin, Credits shows use per day and per member. Your balance lives on Village's platform.

Questions platform leads ask

My founders won't open another platform.

They don't have to live in it. The weekly email carries what needs them, and they can answer from it. Intros, messages and asks send their own notices. The first visit is one invite link, a name and a password.

Emails in my name, to people I know?

Nobody outside the community hears about an intro until the member who knows them says yes, and the link goes from that member or, at their request, from the app. Intro, message, ask and weekly emails have one-click unsubscribe.

Can admins read our direct messages?

Not in the app. Anyone with access to your database can, because messages aren't end-to-end encrypted. We say so plainly.

Can it run on our own domain?

Yes. Your host lets you attach your own domain. The app reads its public address from a setting called APP_URL.

What happens when I remove someone?

They're signed out, can't sign back in and leave the directory. You choose whether their posts stay or go. If they're in the Village group, we ask Village to take them out and tell you what happened.

Does Village host it for me?

No. You run your own copy on your own host. Village builds the project and sells the credits that network search uses.

What does the AGPL mean for us?

You may use, change and run the code for free. If you change it and let people use your changed version over a network, you must offer those users your changed source. A plain-language summary, not legal advice.
