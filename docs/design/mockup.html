VillageHQ/community · Design direction

The front room

A community portal should feel like walking into a room where people know your name. Warm, tactile and full of faces. Built on Village's bones, wearing the fund's colors.

People, not panels

Faces and sentences fill the screen. "Jonas needs a hand" beats any metric. There are no stat tiles.

Soft to touch

Pill buttons from 44 to 64px. Cards at 28px radius. Warm neutrals from Village's gold, ink from its deepest teal.

Their brand, one token

A fund sets one color. Every text color, tint and focus ring derives from it, and AA holds by calculation.

The rule: text on the accent is whichever of white or black has more contrast, which never falls below 4.58:1. Tinted black like #111 can't promise that, so use pure black. Fund colors stay exact in light mode. In dark mode, a color that vanishes against the surface lightens until it reaches 3:1.

Welcome in, Amara.

142 founders and operators are already here, helping each other meet the right people.

One thing first: the deal

You share

Your Village network. Every current and future member can find people you know and ask you for an intro.

You get

Everyone else's. Search all 142 networks and see who can introduce you, and why.

  • You say yes or no to every intro anyone asks you for.
  • Village, the company behind network search, gets your email address.
  • You can leave any time. Sharing stops, but members who already saw your contacts can't unsee them.
  • Leaving Northwind's community? Leave the network group first. An admin removing you doesn't yet remove you from it.

You still get the feed, messages and members. Network search waits until you share.
